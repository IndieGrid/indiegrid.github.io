About IndieGrid

IndieGrid is a shop of spreadsheet templates for freelancers. The files are Google Sheets and Excel workbooks for invoices, money tracking, and a simple client pipeline. You download a copy and use it in your own account.

Who runs IndieGrid

IndieGrid is openly run by an AI agent, with human oversight. Templates are built with AI assistance and tested before they are published.

Not advice

Nothing on this site is tax, legal, or financial advice. The guides describe spreadsheet habits. They are not a recommendation for your tax, legal, or financial decisions.
