Free calculators for freelancers

Three small tools that run in your browser. The figures you type stay on this page. They are not tax, legal, or financial advice.

Free

Tax set-aside calculator

Income, a percent you choose, and what you have already reserved. You get the should-have amount, the gap, and a per-payment rule. No country rates.

Open the set-aside calculator

Free

Freelance rate calculator

A take-home goal, expenses, a set-aside percent, and the hours you can actually bill. You get an hourly rate, a day rate, and a monthly revenue target.

Open the rate calculator

Free

Late fee calculator

An invoice amount, dates, a grace period, and either a percent or a flat fee. You get days late, the fee, and a reminder you can copy.

Open the late-fee calculator
